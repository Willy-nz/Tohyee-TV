With this on, Tohyee TV reloads the tab each time someone starts watching, then presses Play if the video doesn’t start by itself. Chrome will ask once for permission to do that on this site.

Advanced

The Tohyee TV tuner must be running on this computer. Picture and sound are only sent while someone is watching in Plex. Closing the tab, or clicking Stop, ends the broadcast.
