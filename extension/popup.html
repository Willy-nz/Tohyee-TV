Some services, like Sky Go, limit how many devices can watch at once, and a paused video still counts. Leaving the page closes the player; Tohyee TV pauses straight away, leaves after the time above, and goes back and presses Play when someone starts watching. Chrome will ask once for permission to press Play and Pause on this site.

Advanced

In reusable-tab mode, open three tabs on the same video website and arm each as a different reusable tab. Plex chooses which saved channel each tab plays.

The Tohyee TV tuner must be running on this computer. Picture and sound are only sent while someone is watching in Plex. Closing the tab, or clicking Stop, ends the broadcast.
