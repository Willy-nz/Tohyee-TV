HOW IT WORKS

Three steps from tab to TV.

1

Install the app

One Windows installer. It includes the encoder and everything else it needs, and sits quietly in your system tray.

2

Broadcast your tab

Open the video in Chrome, click the Spitz icon and choose Broadcast this tab to Plex. You can minimise Chrome or switch tabs.

3

Watch in Plex

Plex sees Tohyee TV as a network TV tuner. Your tab shows up under Live TV as a channel, with its own guide entry.

FEATURES

Built for the living room.

Picture and sound, even minimised

Chrome keeps sending the tab's picture and sound while it's minimised or in the background.

Up to eight channels

Give each channel its own tab, name and number. Stop one and the others keep playing.

Only works when you're watching

Nothing is sent until someone plays the channel in Plex. Several screens watching the same channel share one stream.

Standby frees your stream

When nobody's watching, it closes the video player, so the household can use that streaming slot elsewhere.

Light on your PC

When Chrome already sends a format Plex can play, Tohyee TV passes it straight through instead of compressing it again.

Stays at home

Runs on your own computer and home network. No accounts, no cloud and no router changes.

NEW IN 0.6

A paused video still counts. Standby doesn't.

Many streaming services limit how many devices can watch at once, and a paused player still uses one of those places. Tohyee TV now steps out of the way when nobody's watching.

  1. WATCHING

    Someone plays the channel in Plex, and your tab is live.

  2. PAUSED

    They stop watching. A few seconds later the video is paused.

  3. STANDBY

    Still nobody after a minute (you choose how long)? The tab moves to a standby page and the player closes.

  4. BACK ON

    Someone starts watching again. Tohyee TV goes back to the same video and presses Play.

The service decides how quickly it frees the slot after the player closes. Prefer to just pause, or keep playing? Pick that in the extension instead.

SETUP

Up and running in about ten minutes.

  1. 1

    Download and install

    Run the installer from the latest release. It isn't code-signed yet, so if Windows shows a SmartScreen warning, choose More info, then Run anyway.

  2. 2

    Add the Chrome extension

    In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and pick the folder the app's Connect to Plex page opens. Pin the Spitz icon.

  3. 3

    Connect it in Plex

    In Plex, open Settings, pick your server, then Live TV & DVR and DVR Setup. Enter the tuner address shown in the app, use its guide address for the programme guide, and map the channel.

  4. 4

    Broadcast and watch

    Open your video tab, click the Spitz icon, choose Broadcast this tab to Plex, then play the channel in Plex. The app has a built-in test picture if you want to check Plex first.

You'll need

  • A Windows PC (64-bit)
  • Google Chrome, version 116 or newer
  • Plex Media Server on the same PC or your home network

Already have a DVR in Plex?

Using an XMLTV guide can mean recreating your existing DVR setup. Check Plex's instructions before changing anything.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions

Does it work with every streaming site?+

Not always. Some sites protect their video from capture, and the picture comes through black. Tohyee TV doesn't get around that protection, or around any service's own viewing limits.

Why only Chrome?+

Chrome can capture a single tab with its sound and keep going when the window is minimised. In testing, Firefox gave no sound and stopped when minimised.

Can I rewind in Plex?+

Plex can rewind what it has received since you started watching. Nothing is sent before then, so there's nothing earlier to go back to.

Do I need Plex Pass?+

Plex says recording needs Plex Pass. Live TV features and screen labels vary with your Plex version and account, so check yours if setup is blocked.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?+

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows doesn't recognise the publisher. The download and its checksum are on the project's GitHub releases page.
